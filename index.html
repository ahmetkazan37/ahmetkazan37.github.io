Last Cure: Zombie Survival

The city went silent three years ago. You are the last one left. Find the cure, or see how long you can survive.

Story mode

Explore a ruined city and the wild lands beyond it, raid dark research labs in first person, build up your home base and make the serum that could save what is left.

Wave Mode

Endless first person survival in a walled desert outpost. Loot crates, hide, blow up barrels, fight a boss every tenth wave and grow your survivor profile with permanent upgrades.
