Frequently asked

How do I switch between Story and Wave Mode?
Tap MENU (under the minimap) and choose Back to title. On the title screen you pick Story (Continue) or Wave Mode.

How do I aim while shooting?
Keep your finger on FIRE and slide it. For grenades: hold GRENADE, aim at the red marker, let go to throw.

What are Skulls?
Skulls are earned in every Wave Mode run (waves, kills and bosses). Spend them on permanent upgrades and starting weapons in your profile.

Is my progress saved?
Yes, on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.
