Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains how the game Last Cure: Zombie Survival (the "app"), made by Ahmet Kazan ("we"), handles information.

What the app itself stores

Your game progress, settings and your Wave Mode profile name are stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers, we do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app can show optional rewarded video ads, for example to continue a run. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • device identifiers for advertising (only if you allow it in iOS) and app instance identifiers
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • information about ad views and interactions, and app usage related to ads
  • diagnostic data such as crash and performance information of the ad SDK

We do not use this information ourselves and we do not sell personal information. More about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google Privacy Policy.

The app does not ask permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can limit ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising, and Tracking).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and contains violence and horror themes.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. Removing the app deletes all locally stored game data. For questions about data processed by Google, see the links above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
